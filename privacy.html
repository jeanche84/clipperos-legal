Clipper OS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

What Clipper OS is

Clipper OS is a private, single-operator tool built and run by one individual. It runs locally on that operator's own computer. It is used to prepare short video clips and publish them to a TikTok account owned by the operator. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and no one other than the operator can sign in to it or use it.

Whose data this policy covers

Only the operator's own. Clipper OS authorises exactly one TikTok account — the operator's own account — and acts on behalf of that account. It does not collect, request, receive or process personal data belonging to any other person, and there is no mechanism by which a third party could supply data to it.

What is stored, and where

  • A TikTok access token and refresh token for the single authorised account. These are written to a file on the operator's own computer and are used solely to call TikTok's official Content Posting API on that account's behalf. They are not transmitted anywhere except to TikTok, and they are not stored on any server.
  • The operator's own video files and the captions written for them, held locally on the same computer.
  • A local record of what was published and when, kept so the same clip is never published twice.

What is not done

Clipper OS does not sell, rent, share, transfer or disclose data to anyone. It has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and no third-party processors. It does not read, collect or store information about TikTok users other than the single authorised account holder. It operates no public website or service that anyone else can interact with.

TikTok data

Information Clipper OS obtains from TikTok is limited to what the authorised account's own posting permissions return: the account's username and display name, its available privacy settings, its maximum permitted video length, and the status of content it has submitted. This information is used only to check that a clip may lawfully be posted to that account, and it is not shared with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Everything described above lives on the operator's own computer and is deleted by deleting those files. The operator can revoke Clipper OS's access at any time from TikTok's own settings, under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Doing so immediately ends its ability to act on the account.
