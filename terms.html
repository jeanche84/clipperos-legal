1. What this software is

Clipper OS is private software built and operated by one individual for their own use. It runs on that operator's own computer and is used to prepare short video clips and publish them to a TikTok account that the operator owns.

2. Who may use it

Only its operator. Clipper OS is not distributed, licensed, sold or made available to the public. There is no sign-up, no user account, and no way for a third party to obtain access to it. These terms exist to describe the software's operation, not to offer it to anyone.

3. Accounts it acts on

Clipper OS acts only on a TikTok account that the operator owns and has explicitly authorised through TikTok's official OAuth flow. It authorises one account. It never posts to, reads from or acts on any account belonging to anyone else.

4. Content

All content published through Clipper OS is prepared by the operator, who is solely responsible for it, including for holding the rights necessary to publish it and for its compliance with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. Every clip is reviewed before it is eligible to be published; nothing is published that has not passed that review.

5. Use of the TikTok API

Clipper OS uses TikTok's official Content Posting API and Login Kit in accordance with the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and TikTok's Content Sharing Guidelines. It uses only documented, supported behaviour. It does not attempt to circumvent any restriction, rate limit, visibility rule or review requirement that TikTok applies to it, and where TikTok restricts an unaudited client to private posting, Clipper OS enforces that restriction itself rather than working around it.
